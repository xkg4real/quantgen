# BS-21: discount-factor funding for bond carry

The owner approved this narrowly scoped correction on 2026-10-30. Bond carry
now subtracts the loan repayment implied by the funding discount factor:

```text
h = min(requested horizon, time to maturity)
funding = price * (1 / DF(h) - 1)
carry = coupon * face * h - funding
```

The previous `price * continuous_zero(h) * h` was a first-order approximation.
For a continuously compounded 20% one-year rate and price 98, it charged 19.60;
the discount-implied repayment charges approximately 21.6975. At a simple
contract rate `r`, `DF(h) = 1 / (1 + r*h)` recovers exactly `price*r*h`.
Negative rates remain supported. Zero, negative, or non-finite funding
discount factors are rejected.

This remains a full-price, single-curve funding proxy. It does not introduce an
executable repo quote, repo spread, haircut, settlement/accrued-interest model,
or transaction-cost assumption. Cashflow ageing, roll-down, scenario pricing,
and the existing duration approximation are unchanged. Total carry-and-roll
and its derived breakeven appropriately reflect the corrected funding cost.
The common bond narrative discloses the proxy for both Flet and native Qt.

## Verification

`python -m unittest tests.bond_funding_acceptance -v` uses only synthetic
curves and cashflows. Its eight tests cover independent simple-loan repayment,
exact continuous accumulation, maturity capping, discount/repayment identity
and face scaling, invalid discounts, and the existing holding-period P&L
regression, bootstrapped money-market quotes, and zero-coupon redemption P&L.
The latter two regression oracles preserve the overlapping PR #18's cases;
PR #19 consolidates the fix with disclosure, validation and CI coverage rather
than applying the funding correction twice.
Native adapter, calculator, and export checks use injected fixtures.
The existing bond module's local self-check also covers price/yield round trips,
duration, convexity, key-rate risk, scenario signs, and hedge sizing.

No market data, actual research store, protected cache, account, or holdout is
used by these checks. This change is not a research-gate approval or a live
pilot authorization.
